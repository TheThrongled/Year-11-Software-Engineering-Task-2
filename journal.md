*Designing characters and environment:*

* The Setting: Medieval fantasy setting, the architecture and zietgiest is that of an old, stylised medieval society. However, the technology is highly magical, the society is divided into three classes.

First, there are the mortals who have no magical abilities and are akin to peasants or serfs. They are used for slave labour or servancy to local gu masters. They are not well versed in the secrets of this world.

Second, there are the gu masters. They have awekened there arperture using hope gu. The arpeture is a spiritual singularity located in the abdomen. it contains ones qi, which is akin to life force. In mortals, this life force remains unharnessed. However, from the arpeture gu masters can infuse their primeval essence with gu. Gu are bug-like creatures that contain the essence of the laws of nature and the flow of life.
