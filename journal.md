# Journal

**Day 1-2**

*Designing characters and environment:*

* The Setting: Medieval fantasy setting, the architecture and zeitgiest is that of an old, stylised medieval society. However, the technology is highly magical, the society is divided into three classes.

* The Protagonist: In this magical world you are part of a common gu master (the middle class of people with awakened primeval powers) family. Despite your talents and projected success, you end up having a terrible talent level. However, in this era, a new age has arose due to the invention of the "pill path". Essentially, anything and everything, although living and mythical beings are preferred, can have their essence contained in refined "pills" in which you can consume. (hence the name of the game)

* Environment: The game will revolve around a progression to different areas:
* -{Area 1: Wolf tide.}-
 A bunch of wolf tide in your local mountain invade, because your considered cannon fodder your hired to go into the vanguard. Instead, you discover a cave with a inheritance from an deceased demonic cultivator. You can go on their inheritance and get a bunch of items, finally going to defend against the wolf tide. Crucially, while fighting on the front lines, you fall back into the demonic cave looking to secure the end of your inheritance. There, you find the demonic cultivator's instruction: he was actively siphoning primeval energy from the clan's ancestral grounds via a hidden array, which seems to be suspiciously dense. You find a teleportation token, but it lacks substance. With this in mind, you fight off the last of the wolf tide and become a respected lord.

* -{Area 2: Escape to Central Continent.}-
 The Wolf Tide is repelled, and you are celebrated. However, the Divine Investigator arrives. He is tracking the demonic energy siphoned from the clan's grounds, and he comes with a vengence, aggresively investigating your pill path standing to enforce the major powers rule against the nascent movement. To expose you, the Investigator forces a direct confrontation and corners you. Instead - to escape with your life - you flee back to the Demonic Cave and activate the hidden array using the investigators furious affront towards you (once he discovers the cave) as a catalyst for the token to work. The deceased left you a hook, harnessing clan energy to keep his remnant soul alive and power the teleportation array back to his home blessed land (pocket dimension native to higher ranked masters who reach a new realm of cultivation). He needed a massive burst of energy to complete the spell, which the Divine Investigator's furious attack perfectly provides. The remnant soul of the demonic cultivator manifests, laughs at your "terrible talent," and declares your body will nevertheless suffice as a vessel to return home, however the extreme, unprecedented power that complicates the demonic defector's planned cajoling of your resources forcefully damages everyone. Thus, the divine investigator is blinded, the soul has no time react, and you are super charged into his realm while having to fight off his remaining presence.
