### Project Vision
This project translates strategic thinking, character progression, and RPG style combat into an Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) framework in Python. 

#### Core Gameplay Loop
* **Actions**: Movements are based on direct attack and movement on a turn based level.
* **Strategic Combat**: Enemies have various debuffs and the player's abilities are centred around the dismantling of enemies debuffs. They will eventually kill the player and as the player gets more items he must continually adjust their weapons to get the furthest.
* **Progression**: After defeating enemies using a combination of exploration and combat, there will be new items and "qi" the person gets. With enough qi they can eventually break through and reach new levels, thus making the objective of the game to develop the character in the most efficient way possible.
